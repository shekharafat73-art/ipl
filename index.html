Common Questions

How do I know my payment went through?+
After paying via UPI, click "I've Paid" in the popup and enter your UPI UTR number. Your booking status will update to "Payment Received" and you'll see it on your status page.
How long until I get my ticket?+
Most tickets are delivered within 2 hours of payment confirmation. You'll receive it on your registered mobile number and email.
What if my booking shows "Pending" for long?+
If your status is pending for over 30 minutes after payment, contact us on Instagram @ticketzoneindia with your Booking ID.
Can I get a refund?+
Yes. If we cannot deliver your ticket after payment, 100% refund within 24 hours. If match is officially cancelled by BCCI/IPL, full refund within 48 hours.
